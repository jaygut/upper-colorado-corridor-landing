Separate cases · straight-line distance

The water record lives elsewhere.

Dated measurements exist at North Inlet and East Inlet near Grand Lake, about 100 km to the north-east, and on the Colorado River above Glenwood Springs, about 79 km to the west.

No delivery from the parcel to any of these stations has been traced or tested. The Glenwood station lies downstream on the Colorado River, and map position alone establishes no effect. The next chapters test the method on the stations' own ground.

Grand Lake, Colorado · associated basins

Two basins. One fire perimeter.

The East Troublesome fire perimeter overlaps 27.3% of the basin associated with the North Inlet station, 20.6 km². It overlaps none of the East Inlet basin. Land outside the perimeter is unclassified for this fire, which is different from shown unburned. Imagery appears only inside the two basins.

Retrospective context. The post-fire image dates from 7 Sep 2021 and the perimeter was revised on 16 Oct 2026. Neither existed during the August 2021 storm. A spectral class is a remote-sensing response. It measures no mortality, recovery or biodiversity change.
