The lagged water model did not improve on development-selected persistence. Paired MAE gain -0.048443, with a 95% storm bootstrap interval [-0.880936, 0.798559]. Positive gain means less error. No predictive upgrade is established.

Climatology performed better on the final block. The prespecified comparator remains persistence; the final block is not used to select a replacement.

Full burn-state Tier A remains blocked by missing prefire observations and incomplete disturbance, terrain and managed-connectivity joins. East Inlet remains context only without independent forcing and discharge.

  • Storm windows were selected retrospectively from rainfall. Their start can precede the qualifying 2 mm in 6 h trigger by up to 22.83 h; this is not a validated issued forecast.
  • The 24 h dry-gap event rule makes all 28 antecedent 24 h rain features zero. The fitted B1 comparison therefore varies with lagged discharge and season.
  • One eligible window has 900 s native cadence; 27 have 600 s cadence. Sensor-change quality control remains unverified.
